⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTES:

1. OCR (text extraction) isn't perfect
   → Always review the extracted text
   → Correct any mistakes manually
   → Look for [needs review] markers

2. The website has sample content
   → You need to update script.js with your actual notes
   → Examples are provided to show you the format

3. Keep your original images safe
   → Don't delete the JPG files
   → They're your backup

4. Dependencies may be needed
   → If scripts fail, install: pip install pytesseract pillow python-docx
   → Or just follow the manual guide
